About Arabic Coffee

Arabic coffee, or qahwa arabia, is a traditional Middle Eastern brew celebrated for its unique flavor and cultural importance. Made from lightly roasted arabica beans and often infused with cardamom, saffron, or cloves, it offers a distinctive golden color and a smooth, aromatic taste.

In Saudi Arabia and across the Arab world, Arabic coffee is more than a drink — it's a symbol of hospitality and respect, typically served in small cups (finjan) without sugar, accompanied by dates, and poured from a beautifully crafted dallah.

At Wafi Café, we prepare it the traditional way — rich in aroma, full of heritage, and brewed to perfection for every guest.
